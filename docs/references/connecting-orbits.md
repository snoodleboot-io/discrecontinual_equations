- **[Beyn1990]** W.-J. Beyn. *The numerical computation of connecting orbits in dynamical systems*. IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis 10(3), 379–405, 1990. DOI 10.1093/imanum/10.3.379. Used in: `continuation.connecting_orbit.HomoclinicOrbit`, `HeteroclinicOrbit` (projection boundary conditions on a truncated interval, exponentially small truncation error); `continuation.homoclinic_curve.HomoclinicCurve`; seeded by `continuation.homoclinic_shooting.HomoclinicShooting`. The eigenbasis normalisation (4.4) that HomCont applies is not used here.
- **[Bulsara2004]** A. R. Bulsara, V. In, A. Kho, P. Longhini, A. Palacios, W.-J. Rappel, J. Acebron, S. Baglio and B. Ando. *Emergent oscillations in unidirectionally coupled overdamped bistable systems*. Physical Review E 70, 036103, 2004. DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.70.036103. Used in: `systems.coupled_ring.FluxgateRing` (eq. 2, coupling moved outside the gain), `systems.coupled_ring.FerroelectricRing` (eq. 1 with `a → a + λ`), the heteroclinic birth of the ring oscillation (section III, Fig. 3).
- **[CastroLohse2014]** S. B. S. D. Castro and A. Lohse. *Stability in simple heteroclinic networks in R⁴*. Dynamical Systems 29(4), 451–481, 2014. arXiv:1401.3993, DOI 10.1080/14689367.2014.940853. Used in: `continuation.network_stability.CycleStability.delta` (eq. 3, Lemma 5.1), `CycleStability.is_predominantly_stable` (Theorem 2.6), `NetworkCompetition` (Propositions 5.2 and 5.3, Table 1).
- **[ChampneysKuznetsovSandstede1996]** A. R. Champneys, Yu. A. Kuznetsov and B. Sandstede. *A numerical toolbox for homoclinic bifurcation analysis*. International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos 6(5), 867–887, 1996. DOI 10.1142/S0218127496000485. Report version: *HomCont: an AUTO86 driver for homoclinic bifurcation analysis, version 2.0*, CWI report AM-R9516, 1995. The projection conditions were checked against AUTO-07p's `homcont.f90` routines `PRJCTN` (Schur basis of the transposed Jacobian's stable or unstable subspace, Beyn's (4.4) normalisation) and `FBHO` (`L_s (u(0) − x₀) = 0`). Used in: `continuation.homoclinic_curve.HomoclinicCurve` (the defining system: collocation, equilibrium, projection, phase, one free parameter), `continuation.connecting_orbit`.
- **[DoedelKooiKuznetsovVanVoorn2008]** E. J. Doedel, B. W. Kooi, Yu. A. Kuznetsov and G. A. K. van Voorn. *Continuation of connecting orbits in 3D-ODEs: (I) Point-to-cycle connections*. International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos 18, 1889–1903, 2008. arXiv:0706.1688, DOI 10.1142/S0218127408021439. Used in: `continuation.heteroclinic_network.HeteroclinicNetwork.solve` (eq. 5a, `L⁻(u(τ₋) − ξ) = 0`), `continuation.homoclinic_shooting.HomoclinicShooting.gap` (eq. 6, start `ξ + εv`), the truncation error bound `C exp(−2 min(μ₋|τ₋|, μ₊|τ₊|))` behind `ConnectingOrbit.estimate_orbit_error`.
- **[GarridoDaSilvaCastro2019]** L. Garrido-da-Silva and S. B. S. D. Castro. *Stability of quasi-simple heteroclinic cycles*. Dynamical Systems 34(1), 14–39, 2019. arXiv:1606.02592, DOI 10.1080/14689367.2018.1445701. Used in: `continuation.network_stability.transition_matrices` (eqs. 3 and 7, Assumption A), `stability_indices` (Lemmas 3.2 and 3.5, Theorems 3.4 and 3.10), `f_index` (Lemma 3.9, Appendix A); Rock-Scissors-Paper check (Lemma 4.1).
- **[Gaunersdorfer1992]** A. Gaunersdorfer. *Time averages for heteroclinic attractors*. SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 52(5), 1476–1489, 1992. DOI 10.1137/0152085. Checked in the IIASA preprint WP-91-11 (1991). Used in: `continuation.heteroclinic_network.CycleSpectrum.residence_ratios` (Lemma 1, Remark 2).
- **[GonchenkoKazakovTuraevShilnikov2022]** S. Gonchenko, A. Kazakov, D. Turaev and A. L. Shilnikov. *Leonid Shilnikov and mathematical theory of dynamical chaos*. Chaos 32, 010402, 2022. arXiv:2112.02423, DOI 10.1063/5.0080836. Used in: `continuation.shilnikov.SaddleFocus.satisfies_shilnikov_criterion` (section II: eigenvalues `λ ± iω`, `γ` with `λ < 0 < γ` and the condition `λ + γ > 0`).
- **[GuckenheimerHolmes1988]** J. Guckenheimer and P. Holmes. *Structurally stable heteroclinic cycles*. Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society 103, 189–192, 1988. DOI 10.1017/S0305004100064732. Used in: `systems.reflection_cubic.guckenheimer_holmes`.
- **[KirkSilber1994]** V. Kirk and M. Silber. *A competition between heteroclinic cycles*. Nonlinearity 7(6), 1605–1621, 1994. DOI 10.1088/0951-7715/7/6/005.
