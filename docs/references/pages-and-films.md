## Pages, films and numerics infrastructure

- **[Brent1973]** R. P. Brent. *Algorithms for Minimization without Derivatives*. Prentice-Hall, Englewood Cliffs, NJ, 1973. Used in: `webplot.manifold_flow.flow_branches` (chs. 3-4, the zero finder behind `scipy.optimize.brentq`, locating where a branch leaves the box on its dense output).
- **[Catmull1974]** E. Catmull, R. Rom. *A class of local interpolating splines*. In R. E. Barnhill, R. F. Riesenfeld (eds.), Computer Aided Geometric Design, Academic Press, 1974, pp. 317-326. DOI 10.1016/B978-0-12-079050-0.50020-5. Used in: the page scripts of `webplot.renderer.D3Renderer`, `webplot.stage_renderer.StageRenderer` and the stochastic stage renderer, which draw branches and cycle curves as Catmull-Rom splines through the computed points.
- **[Dormand1980]** J. R. Dormand, P. J. Prince. *A family of embedded Runge-Kutta formulae*. Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics 6(1), 19-26, 1980. DOI 10.1016/0771-050X(80)90013-3. Used in: `webplot.manifold_flow.flow_branches` (the RK5(4) tableau and embedded error weights, identical to SciPy's `RK45`).
- **[Hairer1993]** E. Hairer, S. P. Nørsett, G. Wanner. *Solving Ordinary Differential Equations I: Nonstiff Problems*, 2nd rev. ed. Springer, 1993. DOI 10.1007/978-3-540-78862-1. Used in: `webplot.manifold_flow.flow_branches` (§II.4, the starting step size and the step-size control).
- **[Shampine1986]** L. F. Shampine. *Some practical Runge-Kutta formulas*. Mathematics of Computation 46(173), 135-150, 1986. DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-1986-0815836-3. Used in: `webplot.manifold_flow.flow_branches` (the free quartic interpolant of the Dormand-Prince pair, used for dense output and the box crossing).
- **[Takens1981]** F. Takens. *Detecting strange attractors in turbulence*. In D. Rand, L.-S. Young (eds.), Dynamical Systems and Turbulence, Warwick 1980, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 898, Springer, 1981, pp. 366-381. DOI 10.1007/BFb0091924. Used in: `plot.delay_plot.DelayPlot` (Theorems 1 and 2, delay embeddings of a diffeomorphism and of a flow into 2d + 1 dimensions).
- **[Yuksel2009]** C. Yuksel, S. Schaefer, J. Keyser. *On the parameterization of Catmull-Rom curves*. 2009 SIAM/ACM Joint Conference on Geometric and Physical Modeling, pp. 47-53, 2009. DOI 10.1145/1629255.1629262. Used in: the same page scripts as [Catmull1974], which use the centripetal parameterization (`d3.curveCatmullRom.alpha(0.5)`), for cubic curves the one member of the family guaranteed to form no cusp or self-intersection within a segment (their main result).
