## Stochastic solvers and iterated integrals

- **[BurrageBurrage1996]** K. Burrage and P. M. Burrage. *High strong order explicit Runge-Kutta methods for stochastic ordinary differential equations*. Applied Numerical Mathematics 22(1-3), 81-101, 1996. DOI 10.1016/S0168-9274(96)00027-X. Used in: `solver.stochastic.srk5.srk5_solver.SRK5Solver`, which cites it for why a stage count is not a stochastic order. Metadata checked by DOI; the text was not fetched.
- **[GottwaldMelbourne2021]** G. A. Gottwald and I. Melbourne. *Simulation of non-Lipschitz stochastic differential equations driven by α-stable noise: a method based on deterministic homogenisation*. Multiscale Modeling & Simulation 19, 665-687, 2021. arXiv:2004.09914. Used in: `solver.stochastic.gottwald_melbourne` (the Thaler map (2.1)-(2.2), $d_\alpha$ and $g_\alpha$ (2.4)-(2.5), the observable and the fast-slow map (4.2) of §4.1, Theorem 4.1, Remark 4.4). Errata, both in v1: §4.1 prints the sign as the running product $\chi_{n-1}\cdots\chi_0$, while the proof of Theorem 4.1 (§5.3) needs one independent sign per excursion. Theorem 5.2 prints $\eta = (c_1+c_2)\,g_\alpha^{-1/\alpha}$, but the stable law's tails give $\eta = ((c_1+c_2)\,g_\alpha)^{1/\alpha}$.
- **[KastnerRossler2022]** F. Kastner and A. Rößler. *An analysis of approximation algorithms for iterated stochastic integrals and a Julia and MATLAB simulation toolbox*. Numerical Algorithms, 2022. DOI 10.1007/s11075-022-01401-z; arXiv:2201.08424. Used in: `solver.stochastic.wiener.LevyAreaSampler.terms` (eqs. (64)-(67), the truncation for strong order 1.0), `MilsteinConfig.levy_area_accuracy`, `iterated_integrals.IteratedIntegralSampler` ((64) with $\gamma = 3/2$), and Theorem 2 (Mrongowius-Rößler against Wiktorsson).
- **[KloedenPlaten1992]** P. E. Kloeden and E. Platen. *Numerical Solution of Stochastic Differential Equations*. Springer, 1992. DOI 10.1007/978-3-662-12616-5. Used in: `srk2_solver` (eq. (11.1.7)), `srk3_solver` (eqs. (11.2.1), (10.4.1), (10.4.6)), `srk4_solver` (eqs. (15.1.4), (14.2.1)), `milstein_solver` (diagonal noise), `iterated_integrals.IteratedIntegralPath` (Chen's relation). Metadata checked by DOI. The book was not to hand, so its equation numbers are the ones the derivations in the code were written against.
- **[KloedenPlatenWright1992]** P. E. Kloeden, E. Platen and I. W. Wright. *The approximation of multiple stochastic integrals*. Stochastic Analysis and Applications 10(4), 431-441, 1992. DOI 10.1080/07362999208809281. Used in: `wiener.LevyAreaSampler` (the truncated Fourier series of the Brownian bridge). Metadata checked by DOI.
- **[Kuznetsov2018]** D. F. Kuznetsov. *To numerical modeling with strong orders 1.0, 1.5, and 2.0 of convergence for multidimensional dynamical systems with random disturbances*. Automation and Remote Control 79(7), 1240-1254, 2018. DOI 10.1134/S0005117918070056; arXiv:1802.00888 (v6, 2022, which the code follows). Used in: `iterated_integrals.IteratedIntegralSampler` (Theorem 1; eqs. (42)-(44), (49), (52), (55)-(61), (63), (66), (67); Algorithm 1), `srk3_solver.SRK3Solver` (eq. (5), the several-channel Taylor scheme). Erratum: (65) prints $0.0236084\,(T-t)^4$. The exact value is $234761/10245312 = 0.0229140$, so the printed constant is a summation slip. The code does not use (65).
- **[Maruyama1955]** G. Maruyama. *Continuous Markov processes and stochastic equations*. Rendiconti del Circolo Matematico di Palermo 4, 48-90, 1955. DOI 10.1007/BF02846028. Used in: `euler_maruyama_solver.EulerMaruyamaSolver`. Metadata checked by DOI.
- **[Milstein1995]** G. N. Milstein. *Numerical Integration of Stochastic Differential Equations*. Kluwer, 1995. DOI 10.1007/978-94-015-8455-5. Used in: `milstein_solver.MilsteinSolver`. Metadata checked by DOI.
- **[MrongowiusRossler2021]** J. Mrongowius and A. Rößler. *On the approximation and simulation of iterated stochastic integrals and the corresponding Lévy areas in terms of a multidimensional Brownian motion*. arXiv:2101.09542, 2021. Used in: `wiener.LevyAreaSampler` (Section 5.1, steps 2-4; Theorem 4.4; Corollary 4.5; eq. (36)), `wiener.WienerIncrements.iterated_integrals` (eq. (7), the index convention), `milstein_solver.MilsteinSolver`, `ensemble.StochasticEnsemble`.
- **[Rossler2010]** A. Rößler. *Runge-Kutta methods for the strong approximation of solutions of stochastic differential equations*. SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis 48(3), 922-952, 2010. DOI 10.1137/09076636X. Used in: `srk3_solver.SRK3Solver` and `srk5_solver.SRK5Solver`, as context only. Its order 1.5 methods are for scalar, diagonal and additive noise (abstract), so they are not the several-channel form, and no `srk*` solver implements its methods. Checked against the abstract by DOI.
- **[TalayTubaro1990]** D. Talay and L. Tubaro. *Expansion of the global error for numerical schemes solving stochastic differential equations*. Stochastic Analysis and Applications 8(4), 483-509, 1990. DOI 10.1080/07362999008809220. Used in: `srk4_solver.SRK4Solver`, for the extrapolation route to higher weak order. Metadata checked by DOI.
- **[Wiktorsson2001]** M. Wiktorsson. *Joint characteristic function and simultaneous simulation of iterated Itô integrals for multiple independent Brownian motions*. Annals of Applied Probability 11(2), 470-487, 2001. DOI 10.1214/aoap/1015345301. Used in: `wiener.LevyAreaSampler` and `milstein_solver.MilsteinSolver`, as the algorithm Mrongowius-Rößler refines (read through [KastnerRossler2022], §3.3 and Theorem 2).
- **[ZhanDuanLiLi2020]** Q. Zhan, J. Duan, X. Li and Y. Li. *Symplectic method for Hamiltonian stochastic differential equations with multiplicative Lévy noise in the sense of Marcus*. arXiv:2010.07691, 2020. Used in: `zhan_duan_li_li` for the name only. The paper's scheme is a symplectic Euler scheme (its (9), Theorems 3.2 and 4.1). The solver's theta-weighted predictor-corrector step is not taken from the paper.
