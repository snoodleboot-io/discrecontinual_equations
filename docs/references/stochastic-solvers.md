- **[BurrageBurrage1996]** K. Burrage and P. M. Burrage. *High strong order explicit Runge-Kutta methods for stochastic ordinary differential equations*. Applied Numerical Mathematics 22(1-3), 81-101, 1996. DOI 10.1016/S0168-9274(96)00027-X. Used in: `solver.stochastic.srk5.srk5_solver.SRK5Solver`, which cites it for why a stage count is not a stochastic order. Metadata checked by DOI; the text was not fetched.
- *DennisSchnabel1983*: full entry, with its uses here, under [Lyapunov exponents, covariant vectors and noise](#lyapunov-exponents-covariant-vectors-and-noise).
- **[GottwaldMelbourne2021]** G. A. Gottwald and I. Melbourne. *Simulation of non-Lipschitz stochastic differential equations driven by α-stable noise: a method based on deterministic homogenisation*. Multiscale Modeling & Simulation 19(2), 665-687, 2021. DOI 10.1137/20M1333183; arXiv:2004.09914. Used in: `solver.stochastic.gottwald_melbourne` (the Thaler map (2.1)-(2.2), $d_\alpha$ and $g_\alpha$ (2.4)-(2.5), the observable and the fast-slow map (4.2) of §4.1, Theorem 4.1, Remark 4.4). Errata, both in v1: §4.1 prints the sign as the running product $\chi_{n-1}\cdots\chi_0$, while the proof of Theorem 4.1 (§5.3) needs one independent sign per excursion. Theorem 5.2 prints $\eta = ((c_1+c_2)\,g_\alpha)^{-1/\alpha}$, but the stable law's tails give $\eta = ((c_1+c_2)\,g_\alpha)^{1/\alpha}$: the exponent's sign is wrong.
- **[Higham2000]** D. J. Higham. *Mean-square and asymptotic stability of the stochastic theta method*. SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis 38(3), 753-769, 2000. DOI 10.1137/S003614299834736X. Used in: `zhan_duan_li_li_solver.ZhanDuanLiLiSolver`, to say what the solver is not: the stochastic theta method of eq. (1.2) has an implicit drift and evaluates the diffusion only at `X_n`.
- **[KastnerRossler2022]** F. Kastner and A. Rößler. *An analysis of approximation algorithms for iterated stochastic integrals and a Julia and MATLAB simulation toolbox*. Numerical Algorithms 93(1), 27-66, 2023 (online 2022). DOI 10.1007/s11075-022-01401-z; arXiv:2201.08424. Used in: `solver.stochastic.wiener.LevyAreaSampler.terms` (eqs. (64)-(67), the truncation for strong order 1.0), `MilsteinConfig.levy_area_accuracy`, `iterated_integrals.IteratedIntegralSampler` ((64) with $\gamma = 3/2$), and Theorem 2 (Mrongowius-Rößler against Wiktorsson).
- **[KloedenPlaten1992]** P. E. Kloeden and E. Platen. *Numerical Solution of Stochastic Differential Equations*. Springer, 1992. DOI 10.1007/978-3-662-12616-5. Used in: `srk2_solver` (eq. (11.1.7)), `srk3_solver` (eqs. (11.2.1), (10.4.1), (10.4.6)), `srk4_solver` (eqs. (15.1.4), (14.2.1)), `milstein_solver` (diagonal noise), `iterated_integrals.IteratedIntegralPath` (Chen's relation). Metadata and chapter titles checked by DOI (ch. 10 strong Taylor, ch. 11 explicit strong, ch. 14 weak Taylor, ch. 15 explicit and implicit weak approximations). The text was not to hand, so the equation numbers within those chapters are the ones the derivations in the code were written against, not checked against the book. Also used in: `stochastic.NoiseConvention`, `stochastic.Stratonovich` (Ch. 4, "Stochastic Differential Equations", pp. 103-160: Stratonovich equations and their Itô drift f + ½ g g′). Chapter title and pages checked against the publisher's record; the full text was not available to us, so no section or equation number is cited.
- **[KloedenPlatenWright1992]** P. E. Kloeden, E. Platen and I. W. Wright. *The approximation of multiple stochastic integrals*. Stochastic Analysis and Applications 10(4), 431-441, 1992. DOI 10.1080/07362999208809281. Used in: `wiener.LevyAreaSampler` (the truncated Fourier series of the Brownian bridge). Metadata checked by DOI.
- **[Kuznetsov2018]** D. F. Kuznetsov. *To numerical modeling with strong orders 1.0, 1.5, and 2.0 of convergence for multidimensional dynamical systems with random disturbances*. arXiv:1802.00888v6, 2022; published as *On numerical modeling of the multidimensional dynamic systems under random perturbations with the 1.5 and 2.0 orders of strong convergence*, Automation and Remote Control 79(7), 1240-1254, 2018. - **[Rossler2010]** A. Rößler. *Runge-Kutta methods for the strong approximation of solutions of stochastic differential equations*. SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis 48(3), 922-952, 2010.
