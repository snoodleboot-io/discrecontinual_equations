- **[Bashforth1883]** F. Bashforth, with J. C. Adams. *An Attempt to Test the Theories of Capillary Action by Comparing the Theoretical and Measured Forms of Drops of Fluid. With an Explanation of the Method of Integration Employed in Constructing the Tables which Give the Theoretical Forms of Such Drops, by J. C. Adams*. Cambridge University Press, 1883. Internet Archive `attempttotestthe00bashuoft`. Read: p. 18, Adams's step formula `y_1 - y_0 = w {q_0 + 1/2 Δq_0 + 5/12 Δ²q_0 + 3/8 Δ³q_0 + 251/720 Δ⁴q_0 + ...}`, with `q = dy/dt` and the differences taken over the values already computed. Stopping after `Δq_0` gives the Adams-Bashforth 2 weights `(3/2, -1/2)`; stopping after `Δ²q_0` gives the Adams-Bashforth 3 weights `(23/12, -16/12, 5/12)`. Used in: `adams_bashforth2_solver.AdamsBashforth2Solver.solve`, `adams_bashforth3_solver.AdamsBashforth3Solver.solve`.

- **[BrankinGladwellShampine1991]** R. W. Brankin, I. Gladwell, L. F. Shampine. *RKSUITE: a suite of Runge-Kutta codes for the initial value problem for ODEs*, Release 1.0, November 1991. It showed that the repository's eighth-order weight `b8[7] = 561292735/797845732` should be `561292985/797845732`, and that the repository's `b9` weights were the seventh-order ones; both fixed in DEQ-139, and every coefficient now agrees with `CONST` to one rounding (re-fetched from netlib.org 2026-11-24). Used in: `prince_dormand87_solver.PrinceDormand87Solver`.
- **[Butcher1963]** J. C. Butcher. *Coefficients for the study of Runge-Kutta integration processes*. Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society 3(2):185-201, 1963. DOI: 10.1017/S1446788700027932. - **[CashKarp1990]** J. R. Cash, A. H. Karp. *A variable order Runge-Kutta method for initial value problems with rapidly varying right-hand sides*. ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software 16(3):201-222, 1990. DOI: 10.1145/79505.79507. We did not read the paper: the publisher refused automated access. The bibliographic data comes from Crossref. - **[DormandPrince1980]** J. R. Dormand, P. J. Prince. *A family of embedded Runge-Kutta formulae*. Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics 6(1):19-26, 1980. The seven-stage 5(4) tableau was checked against [HairerWanner1996], whose routine `CDOPRI` gives "Runge-Kutta coefficients of Dormand and Prince (1980)", and against the Wikipedia article *Dormand-Prince method* (accessed 2026-11-24). - **[Euler1768]** L. Euler. *Institutionum calculi integralis*, vol. 1. St. Petersburg, 1768 (Eneström E342). Read in I. Bruce's English translation, *Concerning the approximate integration of differential equations*, part I, section II, chapter VII, at 17centurymaths.com (`contents/euler/intcalvol1/part2ch7.pdf`), which reprints the Latin alongside. Problem 85, section 650: with `dy/dx = V`, set `V = A` at `(a, b)` and step to `b' = b + A (a' - a)`, then repeat. Sections 652-653: the per-step errors add up, and they are largest where `V` varies most. The repository evaluated `V` at the new abscissa `a'` and the old `y`, not at `(a, b)`, until DEQ-141. Used in: `euler_solver.EulerSolver.solve`.
- **[Fehlberg1969]** E. Fehlberg. *Low-order classical Runge-Kutta formulas with stepsize control and their application to some heat transfer problems*. NASA Technical Report R-315, George C. Marshall Space Flight Center, July 1969. NTRS 19690021375. Read: eqs. (2)-(3), the formula pair (a fourth-order `y` to advance, a fifth-order `ŷ` whose difference from `y` estimates the error), and Table III, "Coefficients for RK4(5), Formula 2" (p. 13). All of the repository's coefficients match Table III. The repository advances with the fifth-order `ŷ`, not Fehlberg's fourth-order `y`. The code's docstring cited Fehlberg's NASA TR R-287 (1968), whose title names fifth- to eighth-order formulas; the coefficients used are those of R-315. Used in: `fehlberg_solver.FehlbergSolver`.

- **[HairerNorsettWanner1993]** E. Hairer, S. P. Nørsett, G. Wanner. *Solving Ordinary Differential Equations I: Nonstiff Problems*, 2nd ed. - **[HairerWanner1996]** E. Hairer, G. Wanner. *DOPRI5*, Fortran code, version of April 25, 1996 (last correction August 8, 2005). Its header says it is "described in" Hairer, Nørsett and Wanner, *Solving Ordinary Differential Equations I*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1993. Read: the copy vendored in SciPy v1.10.0, `scipy/integrate/dop/dopri5.f`. Routine `CDOPRI` gives the coefficients. Routine `DOPCOR` gives the step-size control: the error norm `ERR = sqrt(mean((err_i/sk_i)^2))` with `sk_i = atol + rtol max(|y_i|, |y1_i|)`, then `FAC11 = ERR**EXPO1` with `EXPO1 = 0.2 - 0.75 BETA`, then `FAC = FAC11 / FACOLD**BETA` ("Lund-stabilization"), with `BETA` defaulting to 0.04, `SAFE` to 0.9 and the ratio of new to old step held in `[0.2, 10]`. Also the last-step rule `X + 1.01 H - XEND`, the step after a rejection held to `MIN(ABS(HNEW), ABS(H))`, and the exit "STEP SIZE TOO SMALL" when `0.1 ABS(H) <= ABS(X) UROUND`. Until DEQ-140 the repository's controller was `h S err^-(1+beta)/q` with a max norm, using no previous error; it is now this one (re-read in the copy vendored in SciPy v1.11.4). The defaults `safety_factor = 0.9` and `beta = 0.04` come from here. Used in: `embedded_runge_kutta.EmbeddedRungeKuttaSolver` (`error_ratio`, `accepted_step_size`, `rejected_step_size`, `solve`), and through it `dormand_prince_solver`, `cash_karp_solver`, `fehlberg_solver` and `prince_dormand87_solver` (with its deprecated alias `dormand_prince89_solver`), whose own `_compute_new_step_size` and `_accept_step` held the old controller before DEQ-140.

- **[NumPyDevelopers2026]** NumPy Developers. *numpy.arange*. NumPy Reference, stable, accessed 2026-11-25. https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/generated/numpy.arange.html. - **[PrinceDormand1981]** P. J. Prince, J. R. Dormand. *High order embedded Runge-Kutta formulae*. Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics 7(1):67-75, 1981. DOI: 10.1016/0771-050X(81)90010-3. We did not read the paper: the publisher refused automated access. The bibliographic data comes from Crossref. The thirteen-stage 8(7) tableau was checked against [BrankinGladwellShampine1991], which cites this paper for it, and by solving the order conditions in exact rational arithmetic. We did not check that paper; RKSUITE attributes this tableau to the 1981 paper. Used in: `prince_dormand87_solver.PrinceDormand87Solver`, and its deprecated alias `dormand_prince89_solver.DormandPrince89Solver`.
