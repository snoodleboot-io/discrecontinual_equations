- **[DahiyaCameron2018a]** D. Dahiya and M. Cameron. *Ordered Line Integral Methods for Computing the Quasi-Potential*. Journal of Scientific Computing 75(3), 1351-1384, 2018. arXiv:1706.07509 / doi:10.1007/s10915-017-0590-9. Read in arXiv:1706.07509v2, whose equation numbers are the ones cited. Used in: `continuation.quasipotential.OrderedLineIntegral` (the OLIM-MID update, eqs. (14), (15) and (B-7), with the triangle's stationarity condition along the base, eq. (B-8), solved there by Wilkinson's hybrid secant/bisection method; the geometric action, eq. (2), and the Hamilton-Jacobi equation, eq. (3)), `continuation.quasipotential.CycleAttractor` (the start next to a limit cycle, Sec. 2.1.2, eqs. (7)-(12), used until DEQ-151 and replaced there: it takes the rotational part of the drift parallel to the drift at the nearest point of the cycle, which is wrong at leading order where the rotation turns across the cycle); test systems SDE (21)-(22) and (24); the update-radius rule of thumb, eq. (28), offered as an opt-in by `continuation.quasipotential.default_radius`; the default radius stays 8. Departure: every triangle update is tried, not the paper's hierarchical subset, which doubled the rotating Ornstein-Uhlenbeck error here.
- **[DahiyaCameron2018b]** D. Dahiya and M. Cameron. *An Ordered Line Integral Method for computing the quasi-potential in the case of variable anisotropic diffusion*. Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena 382-383, 33-45, 2018. arXiv:1806.05321 / doi:10.1016/j.physd.2018.07.002. Read in arXiv:1806.05321. Used in: `continuation.quasipotential.OrderedLineIntegral` (the one-point and triangle updates with the metric `A = D^{-1}` at each midpoint, eq. (19), and `b` and `A` interpolated linearly along the base, eqs. (21)-(22); the exact derivative of the triangle's action along the base, eq. (23), with the change of `b` and `A` included, solved by Wilkinson's hybrid secant/bisection method (their ref. [31], Stanford CS-60, 1967; not read here) - used by the opt-in parabolic update, while the default straight base holds `b` and `A` fixed at the current `s` instead, a measured departure; the warning that `U` need not be smooth across the front, Sec. 3.3.2), `continuation.quasipotential.EquilibriumAttractor` (the start from the quasi-potential of the linearisation, eqs. (14)-(18)).
- **[FreidlinWentzell2012]** M. I. Freidlin and A. D. Wentzell. *Random Perturbations of Dynamical Systems*. Springer, Grundlehren der mathematischen Wissenschaften, 2012. doi:10.1007/978-3-642-25847-3 (3rd edition). Only the bibliographic record was checked; the book was not read in review, and nothing in it is cited by number. Used in: `continuation.quasipotential` (the action functional and the quasipotential as the least action to reach a point from an attractor). Normalisation: the library writes `dx = f dt + G dW` with no small parameter, so its `U` is the quasipotential at `eps = 1`; a linear system has `U = (1/2) x^T S^{-1} x` with `A S + S A^T + G G^T = 0`. The original ticket (DEQ-31) wrote `x^T S^{-1} x`, twice too large.

- **[LinYuZhou2019]** L. Lin, H. Yu and X. Zhou. *Quasi-Potential Calculation and Minimum Action Method for Limit Cycle*.
