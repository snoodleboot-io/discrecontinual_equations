- **[CabreFontichDeLaLlave2003]** X. Cabré, E. Fontich and R. de la Llave. *The parameterization method for invariant manifolds I: Manifolds associated to non-resonant subspaces*. Indiana Univ. Math. J. 52(2), 283–328, 2003. DOI 10.1512/iumj.2003.52.2245. Read in the journal copy on X. Cabré's page (web.mat.upc.edu/xavier.cabre/docs/indi1jour.pdf). Used in: `manifold.TaylorManifold.compute` (the invariance equation (1.4), `F ∘ K = K ∘ R`; flows through time-t maps, Remark 6, pp. 292–293, announced on p. 288; the library's flow form `f(P(θ)) = DP(θ) Λθ` is its own infinitesimal version; the non-resonance condition (4) of Theorem 1.1 together with (1.7), which allows a linear `R`; Remark 2, where the jet equations reduce to linear (Sylvester) equations).
- **[Doedel2006]** E. J. Doedel. *Numerical Analysis of Nonlinear Equations*. Lecture notes, Kyoto, July 2006, https://www.math.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~kokubu/RIMS2006/doedel.pdf (read; slide numbers are the printed page numbers). Used in: `parameterization.PseudoArclength` (Keller's pseudo-arclength constraint, slides 22 and 25; the bordered Jacobian is non-singular at a regular point, slide 27) and `parameterization.Natural` (folds, where `f_u` is singular, slides 18–20).
- **[HaroEtAl2016]** À. Haro, M. Canadell, J.-Ll. Figueras, A. Luque and J. M. Mondelo. *The Parameterization Method for Invariant Manifolds: From Rigorous Results to Effective Computations*. Applied Mathematical Sciences 195, Springer, 2016. DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-29662-3. Used in: `manifold.TaylorManifold` (background; Chapter 2, Haro and Mondelo, *Seminumerical Algorithms for Computing Invariant Manifolds of Vector Fields at Fixed Points*, pp. 29–73). Only the book's and its chapters' bibliographic records (Crossref) were checked; the text is paywalled, so no equation in it is cited.
- **[KrauskopfEtAl2005]** B. Krauskopf, H. M. Osinga, E. J. Doedel, M. E. Henderson, J. Guckenheimer, A. Vladimirsky, M. Dellnitz and O. Junge. *A survey of methods for computing (un)stable manifolds of vector fields*. Int. J. Bifurcation and Chaos 15(3), 763–791, 2005. DOI 10.1142/S0218127405012533. Read in the Bristol preprint. Used in: `geodesic_level_sets.GeodesicLevelSets` (Sec. 2.1–2.2: the boundary-value problem, eqs. (9)–(10), the test function (11) and the step tests (12)–(13); the Lorenz manifold of Sec. 2.3, grown to geodesic distance 154.75 in 75 bands).
- **[KrauskopfOsinga2003]** B. Krauskopf and H. M. Osinga. *Computing geodesic level sets on global (un)stable manifolds of vector fields*. SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst. 2(4), 546–569, 2003. DOI 10.1137/030600180. Read in the Bristol preprint, whose equation and section numbers are the ones cited. Used in: `geodesic_level_sets.GeodesicLevelSets` (the boundary-value problem (3.1)–(3.3) for a new mesh point, the step test (3.4) on the angle α, mesh adaptation of Sec. 3.3, the shooting implementation of Sec. 3.4; the Lorenz manifold of Sec. 4.5, grown to geodesic distance 151.75). The IJBC survey's reference list prints the volume as "4(2)"; it is 2(4).
- **[OsingaKrauskopf2004]** H. M. Osinga and B. Krauskopf. *Crocheting the Lorenz manifold*. Mathematical Intelligencer 26(4), 25–37, 2004. DOI 10.1007/BF02985416. Read in the authors' accepted manuscript (Bristol). Used in: `geodesic_level_sets.GeodesicMesh.crochet_pattern` (the mesh read as crochet rounds; the model runs to geodesic distance 110.75 in 25,511 stitches).
